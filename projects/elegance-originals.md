## The problem

Elegance Originals is a Germany-based fashion and lifestyle brand built around clean design and high-end apparel, and the products were strong, but the site wasn't matching that quality. Load times were slow, navigation was clunky, and layout components looked outdated next to the brand's actual positioning, all of which was affecting both performance and perception at the exact moment a shopper is deciding whether to trust the brand.

## What I built

### Homepage and product pages rebuilt around the brand

I restructured the theme codebase to be lighter and faster, then rebuilt the homepage and product pages with a modern layout aligned to the brand's actual aesthetic instead of the outdated components that were there before.

### Custom blocks and a custom app extension

I built multiple custom blocks to support dynamic content across the store, and implemented a custom app using Shopify's app extension system to deliver functionality the core theme couldn't handle on its own, without relying on a third-party app for it.

### Fixing what was actually broken

Old, bloated code came out. Sliders that had been buggy got debugged and re-coded for reliability. Unused assets were stripped to streamline the load experience.

### Mobile navigation, redesigned

The mobile menu and navigation were completely redesigned for clarity and responsiveness, so browsing and shopping felt intuitive across devices rather than an afterthought.
