## The problem

House Haven was a new brand with nothing built yet, no product pages, no review system, no FAQ, and no store identity to speak of. Everything had to be built from a blank Shopify install.

## What I built

### A full store, built from scratch

I built the homepage and the store's first two products, an electric space heater and a knitted throw blanket, with full product copywriting, benefit bullets, a "No More Waking Up Cold" section, and feature descriptions written to sell rather than just list specs.

### Reviews and an FAQ, made real

I integrated client-supplied reviews on each product page, and built a custom, admin-editable FAQ section covering the questions customers actually ask, temperature range, whether a remote is included, shipping speed, the money-back guarantee.

### A benefit ribbon that sells at a glance

I built a "Number One Emerging Space Heater" callout with an icon row covering heat output, remote control, outlet type, and adjustable fan, so the key selling points register before a customer scrolls any further.

### Brand and policy setup

Montserrat headings, transparent product renders, and store policies for returns and shipping generated through Shopify's own settings, plus a custom, admin-manageable review bar so the client can manage social proof without touching code.
